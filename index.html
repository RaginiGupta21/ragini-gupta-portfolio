Machine learning · Classification
Telecom Customer Churn Prediction
Which customers leave, why, and what the business should do about it.
In plain English: Predicts which phone customers are likely to cancel their plan, and shows what drives them to leave.


7,043customers studied
80.17%of predictions correct on new data
14×more cancellations on month-to-month plans


Problem
Churn drives revenue loss, but the causes were buried in 21 account and billing fields.
Approach
Cleaned the data (fixed a mistyped billing field, removed 11 invalid rows, one-hot encoded 20+ features), ran EDA, then compared Logistic Regression with a grid-tested Random Forest.
Result
210 trees at depth 11 reached 80.17%, up 1.49 points over baseline. Tenure, monthly charges and contract type led feature importance and became 3 retention recommendations.

Analytics · Segmentation · Power BI
Retail Customer Analytics & Segmentation
Who the best customers are, who is slipping away, and what to cross-sell.
In plain English: Sorts shoppers into groups by how they buy, and finds products that are often bought together.


£7.28Min sales analyzed
354,321valid sales records
£209K+projected extra revenue (modeled)


Problem
One transaction log, no view of customer value or what sells together.
Approach
Cleaned 495,478 rows to 354,321, built RFM features, chose 4 segments with the elbow method and silhouette score, and mined 132 Apriori rules (top lift 19.59).
Result
Three “Champions” drive 8.5% of revenue and 989 at-risk customers are a win-back target. A 2-page Power BI dashboard (KPI cards, segment scatter, revenue trend, slicers) makes it explorable.

Recommendation system · GenAI internship assignment
GlowMatch: Hybrid BeautyTech Recommender
A recommendation engine for skincare and beauty products that works from catalog data alone, combining content-based filtering, semantic search and grounded explanations.
In plain English: Recommends skincare products based on ingredients, skin type and budget, and explains every suggestion.


1,472products, 116 brands
100%of recommendations within budget (250/250 tests)
~4 msto find similar products


Problem
Early-stage beauty platforms have no purchase history, so personalized product discovery has to start from the catalog itself.
Approach
Built a hybrid pipeline on a real Sephora-sourced catalog: TF-IDF ingredient similarity, LSA semantic vector search with a nearest-neighbor index, rule-based personalization by skin type, category and budget, and a mini-RAG generator that retrieves shared ingredients to explain each recommendation.
Result
Evaluated with proxy metrics rather than claimed “accuracy”: 100% skin-type filter compliance and 100% budget satisfaction (250/250 checks). Delivered end to end with a Streamlit demo UI and a FastAPI REST service, and documented the limitations found, including a data-quality bug in the source ingredients field.
